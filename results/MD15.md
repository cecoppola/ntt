## 2. Findings beyond the refit

1. **The part file is not overlapped at size > 1** (§1.2): the old estimates (4.1 min at 2 GB/s) hid 14.6 s of it. At 2 GB/s the target is
   263.5 s with it, not ≈ 245.
2. **`DB_POOL_VMM`'s seed buffers**: + 12.9 GB of host at init at the target that no model had; with the rounds the node is 398.8 GB
   (V1 modelled 399.1 without it — the same number by coincidence: V1 had no early free).
3. **`MN_T_CHUNK_MB=1024` at the target costs ≈ 14 s of modelled wall** (231.1 s against 217.1 s with `MDB_SHIFT_CHUNK_MB` only), all of it
   the chunk rounds' fixed cost `T_ROUND`, which is **assumed** (0.03 s; the aac6 sweep showed no trend above its noise). Over 0.01–0.1 s the
   target is 220.0–270.1 s with both chunks and 215.0–224.7 s with the shift chunk only. With `COMM_SHMEM_ROUND_MB=1024` the pool no longer
   needs `MN_T_CHUNK_MB` (it was adopted to bound the pool: 81.6 → 45 GB); without it the node is 427.3 GB (fits 480). **A measurement of the
   T-chunk rounds' cost on the target (or a 2-node pair at a size with many rounds) decides whether the target should run `MN_T_CHUNK_MB=0`.**
   Not a change here; the default stays the code's.
4. The design table's fastest row that holds the target is chunking `off` (§4) for the same reason (`T_ROUND`); with the rounds even
   chunking off fits 480 GB at 4.25 × 10¹³ (474.5 GB, 4.32 × 10¹³ ceiling) — too close to the budget to recommend.
5. Sensitivities at the target (modelled, no write): the fabric at 50 / 100 / 200 GB/s per APU 273.4 / 231.1 / 210.2 s; 5 µs per message
   237.7 s; the seed wait without the slow path (after S1) 12.8 s instead of 19.1 (224.9 s).

## 6. Open issues

1. **`T_ROUND` is assumed** and now the largest single uncertainty of the target's wall (±30 s over 0.01–0.1 s, §2.3).
2. **The multi-node init** (`shmem_init`, the meshes, the pool's registration) is not in the model (as before); the target's init is the
   one-node law at the leaf's size (16.3 s).
3. **The formatting rate at size > 1** is the one-node value (0.061 s per 10⁹ digits, 4.5 s at the target); the 2-node point gives 0.104 (7.7 s).
4. **576 writers on Lustre**: the prior is one stream; the aggregate (≈ 350 GB/s at 0.6 each) and the stripe layout are the IO agent's (W1, W5).
5. **Checkpoints are not modelled**: the launch line's `BS_CKPT_DIR` / `BS_CKPT_TREE_EVERY=3` tree sets and the `.top` set (W6) write
   during the run; at Lustre rates they may be exposed.
6. The corrections at size 1 are data-dependent (`--corrections`); at size > 1 they do not touch the part file (it is written after T1).
7. `VMM_BS_GROW` (6 GB) is an observed margin, not a traced allocation.
8. After Batch 1 (S1 the fused span, T2 `BS_SEED_TERMS`, R4, C2, K), refit CAL15 on the new runs: `CAL15_RUNS` + `mn_model.py --fit15`.
