# CRASHES_HANGS — crashes, hangs and intermittent defects

Scope: the t_mn_grid "hangs", the multi-node segfault and hang track (ROCr vs libfabric memhooks), the startup segfault under Cray SHMEM, the leaf-length race, the batch-tier copy race, `DIST_LOGN_TEST` corrections, clean-failure and budget checks. Old item IDs resolve through `results/INDEX.md`; raw data and drivers are in `archive:results_raw_2026-10-19/` and `archive:drivers/`. Labels: m measured, mod modelled, a assumed, i inferred.

## Topic summary

- **Current state (check ecalc/README.md; main wins on any difference).**
  - `FI_MR_CACHE_MONITOR=userfaultfd` is the `mnrun.sh` default (S46, adopted 2026-10-11): it removed the ROCr <-> libfabric lock-order deadlock (base 22/105 hangs vs 0/104). `FI_MR_CACHE_MONITOR=disabled` gives silently WRONG exchanges and is refused by `mnrun.sh` and `target_kit.sh`.
  - `ECALC_VMM_BG` (S34): `0` maps the arena synchronously (+20 s per 2-node run, m), `2` (S35, process-wide HIP lock) was dropped as a switch value (W2A A1; it hung 9/9 in the reproducer); default stays the background mapper. The production segfault in `hipMemMap` of `vmm_bg_map` (about 1 % of 2-node runs, 4/422 m) is not reproduced; the uffd default is the working fix hypothesis (a: same cause), watch 4-node real-size runs.
  - `COMM_INIT_EARLY` (S16) was adopted as built-in and its switch removed (W2A A2): SHMEM is initialised before any device mapping.
  - `ECALC_SEGV_TRACE=1` prints a signal-safe trace (S18W); `ECALC_FATAL_*` one fatal path and `ECALC_BUDGET_CHECK` (C314) exist, off by default.
  - Fixed defects: garbage leaf length (A114, `maxidx` race, per-thread buffer), batch-tier odd-node copy race (R, `mem_dev_copy_on` now waits), `mdb_add_shifted` round sizing (T23: the "65 corrections" of `DIST_LOGN_TEST=23` at 4 nodes was this, fixed in 9a746c8).
- **Not defects.** The t_mn_grid 2-node "hang" (A01, T13) was the TCP mesh over 1 GbE Ethernet; the mnaccept `HANG` item was the queue item's own `timeout 1500` (unit step needs about 1500 s). TCP transport no longer exists (W2B B4), so neither can recur; `t_mn_grid` now runs over SHMEM.
- **Open.** E1 (7.7e10 start-up hang, 1 in 31, not reproduced, single sighting); S30's 20 s stall of node 1 (host vs rank, cause of the draw unknown, see `results/NODE_SLOWMODE.md`); G2 soak of the `DBIG_QSEL=1` stall armed but results never recorded; the 1 % segfault is still unproven fixed.
- **Superseded.** T13's lock-order suspicion for the t_mn_grid hang (refuted by A01); G12's attribution of "65 corrections" to the sharded division (refuted by T23).

## §A01 — the t_mn_grid 2-node "hang" on main (task N1)

Verdict: **not a deadlock, not a code bug.** `mnrun.sh 2 ./tests/t_mn_grid` without `COMM_TRANSPORT` used the TCP mesh, and `COMM_HOSTS` held Slurm host names which resolve on aac7 to `bond0`, the **1 Gb/s management Ethernet**, not Slingshot (200 Gb/s). Main 6d80ef1, 2026-10-11, own 2-node job.

## §S46 — crash + hang track (batch 2026-10-11, track T1)

**Question.** The multi-node segfault in `hipMemMap` called from the background VMM mapper `vmm_bg_map` (`ecalc/dbig.c`; §S34) happens in about 1 % of 2-node runs at 6.441e10 digits per node (4/422, m). Candidates: `ECALC_VMM_BG=0` (synchronous mapping, +20 s per 2-node run) and `ECALC_VMM_BG=2` (s35 lock). A plain soak needs about 600 runs per arm, so this track builds a fast reproducer, runs the arms interleaved (aac7, 2 nodes), and soaks 1-node runs on aac6 for the start-up hang seen once in S42.

**Reproducer switches (off by default, digits identical; later removed in W2A A1).** `ECALC_VMM_REPRO=n` makes the mapper unmap/map each chunk n more times (with `DB_POOL_VMM_CHUNK_GB` small); `ECALC_VMM_REPRO_SPIN=us` adds a thread per APU that maps/unmaps a spare chunk every `us` microseconds, concurrent with the exchanges and libfabric registrations. Chosen reproducer **R1**: 1e10 digits, 2 nodes, chunk 0.0625 GiB, `REPRO=8`, `SPIN=1000`. Job A driver (watchdog, hang capture of `ps -L` stacks, Fisher stats, hold handoff) and an aac6 1-node soak; drivers in `archive:drivers/s46/`.

**Findings (m unless marked).**
1. More map calls alone do not segfault (calibration c1, c2: 0 failures in 20 runs, up to about 11 000 `hipMemSetAccess` + `hipMemMap` per APU, mod); concurrency with the rest of the run is the likelier condition (a).
2. With the spinner, `ECALC_VMM_BG=2` hangs inside `hipMemSetAccess -> ... -> KfdDriver::ImportDMABuf -> drmPrimeFDToHandle` (busy in the kernel, about 98 % CPU) holding the mapper and HIP locks: the lock cannot protect against a stuck driver call, and it blocks every wrapped HIP call.
3. **Base-arm hangs are a lock-order deadlock between ROCr's VMM map and libfabric's memory monitor.** Thread 1 (mapper): `hipMemMap -> ... -> MappedHandleAllowedAgent::EnableAccess -> mmap -> ofi_intercept_mmap -> ofi_intercept_handler -> pthread_mutex_lock` (holds ROCr's lock, wants libfabric's). Thread 2 (a compute thread freeing device memory): `hipFree -> ... -> rocr_mm_dealloc_cb` (libfabric's monitor callback, under its lock) `-> DeregisterReleaseNotifier -> pthread_rwlock_wrlock` (wants ROCr's lock). The segfault itself (a SIGSEGV at a small address inside `hipMemMap`, same mapper thread) is hypothesised to be the same interaction without the deadlock (a).
4. Job B crash (4 nodes, 6.441e10/node, 1 in 26 runs): SIGSEGV at about 42 s, faulting-thread frames in `libamdhip64.so.7` at identical offsets to S34's three traces (`+0x3f8624, +0x403b0d, +0x3cbd39, +0x8cf57, +0x8d21d, +0x36bb8e`), then the ecalc mapper frames.
5. `FI_MR_CACHE_MONITOR=disabled`: mn self-test 2097152 of 2097152 points differ, VERIFY FAILED, then SIGSEGV (stale registrations in the MR cache). **Never use.** `kdreg2` is listed by `fi_info -e` but `/dev/kdreg2` is absent on the compute nodes.
6. `FI_MR_CACHE_MONITOR=userfaultfd`: gate 2 nodes 1e9 PASS, digits identical, 18.72 s (base gate 19.55-21.22 s); with R1 PASS, 22.44 s. A gate with `FI_LOG_LEVEL=info` shows "Default memory monitor is: uffd" on both nodes and no fallback line (caveat a: memhooks may still be initialised, so the functional evidence is the 22 -> 0 change).

## §S34 — where the rc 139 segfault is (2026-10-09)

**Finding (m, 3 independent traces, identical offsets):** the multi-node segfault is inside `hipMemMap`, called from `vmm_map_run` (`ecalc/dbig.c`) in `vmm_bg_map`, the background VMM mapper thread (one pthread per APU, started by `db_vmm_arena_alloc` when `first < arena`, released by `db_vmm_bg_release` from `rns_init`). Symbolised: `libamdhip64.so.7` (ROCm 7.2.4) fault at `+0x3f8624`, via `+0x403b0d, +0x3cbd39, +0x8cf57/+0x8d21d, +0x36bb8e`; at about 42 s wall, when the mapper runs against init / level 1 work and SHMEM/libfabric registration. Consequence: `ECALC_VMM_SAFE=2` (a tail-of-thread fix) cannot help.

**Switch `ECALC_VMM_BG=0`** (branch `s34`, off by default, digits unchanged): map the whole arena synchronously, no `vmm_bg_map` thread; cost is the mapping time the thread hid in init.

## §S16 — the intermittent startup segfault of ecalc under Cray OpenSHMEMX on aac7 (Phase 16 S)

**Symptom (m).** 2 nodes, `COMM_TRANSPORT=shmem`, 1e10 digits: task 1 segfaults inside Cray's `shmem_init_thread` in 3 of 23 launches (after `bs: arenas 45.1 GB allocated`); 0 of 24 at 1e9 / `t_comm`; PE 0 then hangs in PMI. At 1e10 the init runs in a process with about 45 GB of VMM address ranges mapped and a busy seed thread; at 1e9 the seeds are done.

**Switch `COMM_INIT_EARLY=1`** (b658922): pool rule and `mn_init` before `mem_sampler_start` / `rns_init`; self-tests stay after. Adopted later as built-in (W2A A2 removed the switch).

**Diagnosis (mod from four faulting frames).** The faulting thread is a ROCm HSA runtime worker (`libhsa-runtime64.so.1` + `libamdhip64.so.7` + `malloc` on a 294 MB rwxp thread stack) jumping through a corrupt pointer (`rip` = 0x21041, 0xfffffffffffff000, 0xffffffffffe00000). Cray OpenSHMEMX 11.8 over libfabric 2.3.1/cxi without `/dev/kdreg2` uses the **memhooks** MR-cache monitor, which binary-patches glibc's `mmap/munmap/madvise/brk/sbrk/mremap` during `shmem_init_thread`; the patch is not atomic against a thread executing those functions at that moment. Tools: `ecalc/tools/segv2.c`, a preload recording the library's SIGSEGV handler and printing the backtrace and maps lines first.

**Rates (m; 1e10 digits, 2 nodes).**

| toolchain | variant | segfaults / launches |
|---|---|---|
| ROCm 7.0.3, Cray OpenSHMEMX 11.8.0, libfabric 2.3.1 | control | **8 / 78 = 10.3 %** (A16 3/23, b1 1/10, b2 0/10, b3 3/25, b4 1/10) |
| same | `COMM_INIT_EARLY=1` | **0 / 55** |
| same | `DB_POOL_VMM=0` | 0 / 10 |
| same | `FI_MR_CACHE_MONITOR=disabled` | 0 / 10 |
| ROCm 7.2.4 | control / `COMM_INIT_EARLY=1` | 0 / 10, 0 / 10 (rate bounded, not measured: P(0 of 10 | 10.3 %) = 0.34) |

Control 8/78 vs early 0/55: one-sided Fisher **p = 1.2e-02**; binomial P(0 of 55 | 10.3 %) = 2.6e-03. Digits identical at 1e9 (2 nodes, 2 runs) and 1e10 (every batch's first run). `mnaccept --only unit,e9,mn` with the switch on ROCm 7.2.4: 15 passed, 1 failed (`t_mn_grid` 2 procs, rc 124 over the TCP transport, the same failure without the switch: §A01). No cost measured (total 53-59 s with and without).

**Trap for any SHMEM-on-cxi setup.** Cray OpenSHMEMX 11.8 / libfabric 2.3.1 (cxi) without `/dev/kdreg2` uses memhooks, which patches glibc's mmap family in place during `shmem_init_thread`; a thread executing those functions then can continue at a garbage address (about 10 % at 1e10 x 2 nodes, 0 at 1e9). Initialise SHMEM before any device mapping, or use `userfaultfd` (see §S46); never call `shmem_init` from a process with live HIP worker threads. The PE hangs in PMI afterwards (launch under `timeout`).

Other findings: `mnaccept.sh`'s `MNRUN_CPUS_PER_TASK=auto` broke every unit step on >= 2 nodes (`scontrol show node` printed one `CPUTot` per node; fixed in bc17d32 with `head -1`); the "killed step poisons the node" trap did not reproduce in 5 killed steps (`scancel <stepid>` after `timeout 300`); a ROCm 7.2.4 build needs `module unload rocm; module load rocm/7.2.4` at launch and the explicit `HIPCC` path (RUNPATH loses to the login's `LD_LIBRARY_PATH`).

## §A114 — A1 (Phase 14, W3): the intermittent leaf / seed-stage defect — root cause, fix, soak

**Evidence.** E1: job 21105, 7.70e10, size 1: a hang right after init (197 threads in `futex_wait_queue`, 2 in `kfd_wait_on_events`; the next line would have followed the `pthread_join` of the seed thread, binsplit.c 1228), 1 in 31 runs, not reproduced in 5. E2: job 21222, `mn e8 size 4` (1e8, 4 node-processes): node 3 printed `leaf P 18385101070989787659 limbs, Q 1466159 limbs` and the level-1 product refused it; the step passed in three other jobs. The garbage length 0xff2500040000020b is uninitialised memory read as a `size_t`.

**Root cause of E2 (i, the value fits).** At size > 1 a batch-tier leaf is copied to device numbers (`db_copy` -> `shift_into` -> `db_norm` -> `maxidx`, dbig.c); `maxidx` scans the four quarters with one OpenMP thread each. The host buffer for the per-block results was a process-wide `g_hred`, allocated on first use inside `flags_reserve` by `if (!g_hred) g_hred = malloc(...)`, unguarded, and `maxidx` read the global twice (as `hipMemcpy` destination, then as scan source). Two threads both seeing `g_hred == 0` both `malloc`; one then scans the other, never-written buffer: heap garbage as a length. It is P not Q and size > 1 because the leaf hand-over's `db_copy(&bs_Pd)` is the first `dbig` operation of a process (first-use), and it is rare because the preceding serialised allocations stagger the threads; at size 1 the same race is possible with a wrong top-level length as symptom (not seen). Not the Phase 12 copy-order race (the D2H copy is host-synchronous).

**Fix (`ecalc/dbig.c`).** `maxidx` copies each quarter into a per-thread stack buffer (`size_t hred[228 * 8]`, 14.6 KB); `g_hred` is gone; `maxidx` also aborts loudly if its result exceeds the limbs scanned. No computed value changes (bit-identical by construction).

**Mechanism measured (m, job 21230, 4 APUs; `tests/t_hred_race`, 20 000 rounds of the old pattern).** Old pattern: **9 884 of 20 000 rounds with more than one allocation, 29 402 garbage reads**; per-thread buffer: **0**, VERIFY OK. In ecalc the race needs a preemption (a scheduling event: idle-node soaks never show it, a loaded batch did).

**Soak (m).** Before-fix binary on s24-26: mn 1e8 size 4 x100, size 3 x30, size 2 x10: **140/140 ok** (so the idle-node rate is < 1/100; T1's failure was 1 in about 4 executions of the mnaccept step under load); after the fix: **140/140 ok**, 0 DIFFERS. E1 (hang after init) is **open**: hypothesis (a): a lost completion of the seed thread's last DMA wait (`mem_dev_copy_wait` does not `hipSetDevice(dev)` before waiting); the gdb-launch soak `h1` (1e9 size 1 x60, 7.7e10 size 1 x8) was running at write-up and its results were not recorded.

## §S18W — `tools/rundriver.sh` and `ECALC_SEGV_TRACE` (branch `s18-w` off main ef22f22)

Two independent, off-by-default pieces; digits unchanged.

- **`tools/rundriver.sh`**: a sourced bash library for fire-and-forget drivers (`rd_init`, `rd_run`, `rd_health`, `rd_disk`, `rd_verify`, `rd_peaks`; `tools/parse_mem.py` ported). `rd_run` backgrounds a command with timeout `max(2*expected_s, expected_s+900)` and a 20 s-period watchdog (SIGTERM, 60 s grace, SIGKILL, never scancel; writes `ALERT`) on a crash regex, no log growth for `STALL_S` (default 900 s), or the wall timeout. `rd_verify` reads only what the ecalc log itself says (s18ab's false DIFFERS came from its own reference compare). Unit tests with fake commands (m): plain finish rc 0 no ALERT; crash text killed with ALERT after 22 s; silent stall (`STALL_S=30`) after 63 s, all well under 2 minutes.
- **`ECALC_SEGV_TRACE=1`** (default 0; `segv_trace.c/.h`, called from `main` after `COMM_RANK` is set): a SIGSEGV/SIGBUS/SIGABRT handler that writes, with `write(2)` only, rank/host, signal, fault address, a `backtrace_symbols_fd` dump and the maps lines of ecalc's own executable (for offline `addr2line`), then restores `SIG_DFL` and re-raises so the exit status is unchanged. `ECALC_SEGV_TEST=1` raises a SIGSEGV right after install (host-only check). `-rdynamic` not used (own frames print as `bin(+0xNNNN)`, library frames resolve). Tested only with a host-only gcc harness (exit 139 with and without the switch; the aac7 build was not run by that agent); README rows added.

## §T23 — the `DIST_LOGN_TEST` 4-node ">64 corrections" defect

**Question.** At 4 nodes, 1e9 digits, `POOL_LOG=29 DIST_LOGN_TEST=23` stopped with "newton_mn_divmod: 65 corrections" (guard `++nc > 64`), `DIST_LOGN_TEST=24` passed. A defect of the sharded division's forced-grid path?

**Verdict.** **Already fixed; no code change.** It was the `mdb_add_shifted` bug fixed by commit 9a746c8 (2026-09-22, during G12): the shifted add sized its rounds from the windows of nodes 0..g-1 instead of g0..g0+g-1, so on the tree's level-1 group [2, 4) it ran zero rounds and the added operand of a gridded product (P = P_l Q_r + P_r) was never added. `DIST_LOGN_TEST=23` is the first cap at which that level's product becomes a grid at 1e9 over 4 nodes (at 24 it is one plane with X added in the CRT). The wrong P surfaced as the correction guard; G12 attributed it to the division. The path from a wrong P to more than 64 corrections was not traced (a).

**Evidence (m; one-commit bracket on aac6, 4 node-processes on one node).** 6fd0bda (G12) and 06638dd (parent of 9a746c8): rc 1, "65 corrections" (also with `ECALC_RES_LOG=1`); **9a746c8: VERIFY OK on every node**. 116fab6 (old division, `NEWTON_DKM=0`): VERIFY OK at 23 and 24. ## §R — the race at the batch tier's level transition (Phase 12, PLAN.md §27 row R; D5)

**The race.** A leaf level with an odd number of nodes carries its last node up with `region_copy` (>= 2^20 limbs: `mem_dev_copy` -> `mem_dev_copy_on` -> one `hipMemcpy(..., hipMemcpyDefault)` with the destination device current). On ROCm 7.2.4 (MI300A) **a device-to-device `hipMemcpy` whose source and destination lie on the copying device returns at once, queued on that device's null stream**, while a cross-device copy is host-synchronous (`tests/t_copy_order`). Nothing waited, and the next level's kernels run on the other three APUs' own streams, unordered against it. At 1e10 over four processes the odd carries stay in the same region at levels 14, 16, 18 (`place_node` round robin at n <= 16 nodes). Exposure 1 (level 16 -> 17, the failure seen): Q of the carried node (34-36 MB) is the shared operand of both level-17 products, read by the scatter kernel on all four APUs 0.1-0.2 ms after the copy was issued while the copy completes 0.2-3 ms after issue; APUs 0-2 read old contents from the limb where their sweep overtakes the copy, the CRT then reconstructs garbage to the top. Exposure 2 (level 18 -> 19): the next level's CRT stripes overwrite the copy's source while it may still be reading (needs a stalled copy engine; never observed). Signature: the leaf's wrong P starts at limb 2^17 + a few pages (131 072, 131 584, 134 400) and wrong Q at that limb + 3 961 286 (+-1) (Q_A of 2.68e8 terms has 3.7e6 zero limbs, Q16 adds 2.3e5). It is not pool growth: the recipe `POOL_LOG=29 RNS_POOL1_GB=3.2213` grows nothing; it only concentrates the carries in one region under four-process contention. Any per-level probe hid the race because it synchronised the devices.

**Evidence (m).** `t_copy_order` (512 MB copy behind a 0.2 s kernel): device 0 -> 2 returned after 0.197 s (host-synchronous, 0 of 5 overwrites); **device 0 -> 0 returned after 0.000 s and a kernel on device 1 overwrote the source before the copy read it in 5 of 5**; with `hipStreamSynchronize(0)` after the copy: 0.191 s, 0 of 5. Reproducer (`v11_d5.sh`, 1e10 at size 4): unfixed batch 1: 12 of 13 identical, one leaf wrong (P from limb 134 400, Q from 4 095 686); copy probe: every same-region copy of levels 14, 16, 18 completed 0.2-48 ms after issue, next level launched 0.1-0.9 ms after issue (UNORDERED in 47 of 52 lines). With `MEM_COPY_NOWAIT=1` (old copy) 12 of 13 identical, one wrong (offset 3 961 287), and the snapshot of the shared operand B equals memory on every APU (the corruption is in the copy chain). Pooled unfixed rate **7 in 52 (13 %)** (2 in 26 here, V had 5 in 26).

**Fix.** `mem_dev_copy_on` (mem.c) waits for its copy (`hipStreamSynchronize(0)` on the copying device); all callers assume completion, the cross-device case already was. Cost nothing measurable (copies of 34-171 MB at four levels). Also fixed: the batch-local grpB tile requested `L` limbs of pool 1 where it uses `EC_NP * L` planes (reached only with `RNS_BATCH_LOCAL_MIN <= 2`).

**Invariant: no pool grows inside a phase.** `rns_dpool` and `pool_get` abort with the memory accounting when an existing pool would grow while a phase runs, unless `RNS_POOL_GROW=1` (counted and logged under `RNS_VERBOSE`); `mem_pool_guard` is cleared around level-0 layout (`t_bs` runs `binsplit_e` twice) and set for the level loop. The defaults never trip it.

**`mnaccept.sh --stress`** (opt-in, about 10 min): 10 runs of 1e9 at size 4 with `RNS_POOL_GROW=1 POOL_LOG=27 RNS_POOL1_GB=0.4 RNS_BATCH_LOCAL_MIN=2 MEM_DPOOL_FILL=1` force pool 1 to grow at the first batch level (the growth line is required in each log; the first attempt with 0.8054 GB grew nothing and correctly failed).

**Gates (m).** With the fix **56 of 56** forced-growth-recipe runs at 1e10 over 4 processes identical to the reference, every node VERIFY OK (four batches, jobs 20913, 20917, 20922 at f80e517, 20956 at fa73163; 0 growth lines), against 24 of 26 unfixed (chance of 56 clean at the unfixed rate 0.865^56 about 3e-4). `--stress` 10/10 in three batches (30/30 identical, 16 growths logged per run). Regression `mnaccept.sh --full --stress` on fa73163: **18 passed, 0 failed in 2096 s**, including 4e10 size 1 identical, total 79.64 s (161 s with write), against Phase 10's 83.0 and Phase 11's 81.5 +- 1.4 s (wall unchanged). New switches `RNS_POOL_GROW`, `MEM_COPY_NOWAIT`, `ECALC_COPY_PROBE`, `ECALC_B_SNAPSHOT` (witness/diagnostic only), new test `t_copy_order` (needs 3 APUs, about 10 s). Open: exposure 2 has no separate witness; a caller wanting an asynchronous copy must use `mem_dev_copy_async` + `mem_dev_copy_wait`.

## §C314 — C3 (Phase 14): A3 clean failure from worker threads; E12 the collective budget check

**A3: one fatal path (`ecalc/fatal.c`, `fatal.h`).** `ec_fatal(code, fmt, ...)`: the first caller (atomic claim) prints one line `ecalc: FATAL [rank r/n host h, APU d, thread t, after <phase>, rc c]: <message>`, flushes and leaves with `_exit(code)` (no atexit or HIP destructors while other threads compute); later callers park forever; a watchdog `_exit`s after `ECALC_FATAL_WAIT` s (60). `ec_fatal_begin/end` wrap a report (`mem_report("GROW"/"OOM")`, used by the in-phase pool guards and `mem_oom`). Codes: 3 (`EC_RC_FATAL`), 6 (`EC_RC_OOM`: allocation failure or pool growth inside a phase), 8 (`EC_RC_BUDGET`); `ECALC_FATAL_ABORT=1` aborts instead. Every `exit(1)` / `abort()` in `ecalc/*.c` was converted (176 by script, 11 by hand); main-thread usage errors, `BS_LAYOUT_ONLY`, `mn_plan.c` and collective restart refusals stay. Test hook `ECALC_FATAL_TEST=1`.

**E12: `ECALC_BUDGET_CHECK=1`** (off by default, after `mem_report("init")`): per rank peak = max(device in use, model planes + arena at the cap) + max(model host terms, VmRSS); ranks allgather and apply the same rule: a node is over when its ranks' peaks sum above `ECALC_NODE_GB` (480) or their "to take" exceeds MemAvailable; a rank is over when its peak exceeds its share (`ECALC_BUDGET_RANK_GB=<r>:<GB>` is a test hook); any over -> every rank prints the figures and `_exit(8)`.

**Tests (m, aac6-spx-b, job 21258).** `ECALC_FATAL_TEST=1` 1e8 size 1: rc 3, exactly **1** FATAL line; 1e9 over 4 processes: every rank rc 3, 4 FATAL lines (one per rank). `POOL_LOG=27` at 9.2e9 over 4 processes (G13d's c1, formerly rc 139 with corrupted chunks): every rank **rc 6** in 7 s with 4 FATAL lines ("plane pool 0 on APU 0 would grow inside a phase, 0.81 -> 1.07 GB"); the same at 1e9 does not trip the guard (VERIFY OK, twice; needs G13d's 2.3e9 per process). E12: `ECALC_NODE_GB=10` 1e9 over 4: every rank rc 8 after init (peak 84.2 GB = device 62.6 + host 21.6; node 336.8 of 10 GB); `ECALC_BUDGET_RANK_GB=2:1`: rc 8 naming rank 2; default budget: rc 0, "largest rank peak 84.2 GB, node budget 480 GB", to take 164.8 of MemAvailable 362.9 GB, digits identical; 1 node: peak 124.6 GB (device 109.1 + host 15.6), digits identical. No log had Segmentation or core dumped.

**Gates (mnaccept on 17b5e375).** unit 8 PASS; e9 size 1 both bases identical (11.37 / 21.83 s); mn e8 sizes 2, 3, 4 and e9 sizes 2, 4: 5 PASS; full 4e10 identical to the reference, total 62.95 s, RECHECK OK in 48 s (job 21262). Total unit,e9,mn: 15 passed, 0 failed (1274 s).
