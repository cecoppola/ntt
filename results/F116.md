## The change

`ecalc/estimate.py` `partial_row(a)`'s standing row ignored the CLI's `--bw`/`--lat`/`--group`/`--layers`/`--taper`
because `mn_model.cache_partial()` always ran against the module-level `TARGET` fabric, overriding only
`TARGET.write_bw` inside its `runw()`. Fixed minimally, behavior-preserving at the defaults:

- `ecalc/mn_model.py`:
  - `_run_cache(..., fab=None)`: defaults to `TARGET` when not given; now calls `run(fab, T / g, g, ...)` instead
    of hardcoding `run(TARGET, ...)` (this was the actual fabric-ignoring call — `cache_partial`'s `runw()` only
    overrode `write_bw` on the global `TARGET`, but `_run_cache` never saw any other fabric).
  - `cache_partial(..., fab=None)`: defaults to `TARGET`; `runw()`'s per-write-rate override/restore now targets
    `fab.write_bw` (not `TARGET.write_bw`), and passes `fab=fab` down to `_run_cache`.
  - `cache_partial_rooms()` / `cache_partial_primes()` were checked — they are fabric-independent (pool-byte and
    memory-layout math only), no change needed there.
  - `mn_model.py`'s own `main()` (`--cache-partial` entry) calls `cache_partial()` with no `fab` arg, so it still
    defaults to `TARGET` — unchanged behavior, still works (`--help` and `import mn_model` both verified).
- `ecalc/estimate.py` `partial_row(a)`: builds `fab = M.Fabric(M.TARGET.name, a.bw, a.lat, group=a.group,
  layers=a.layers, taper=a.taper, write_bw=M.TARGET_WRITE_BW)` (mirrors `target()`'s own fabric construction at
  line ~171) and passes `fab=fab` into `M.cache_partial(...)`. Docstring updated to note the row is now priced at
  the CLI's fabric, matching `target()`'s other rows. `--bw`/`--lat` help text made no claim to fix.
