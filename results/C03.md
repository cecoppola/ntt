## C4 `COMM_SHMEM_RPIPE=1` (comm_shmem.c)
- Acts inside the rounds path (`COMM_SHMEM_ROUND_MB`); no effect without it, with `COMM_OFI`, for exchanges of one round, or for pool-resident exchanges that skip the rounds (X2 vslot).
- `rounder_pipe`: round j+1 opens (receive offsets published, own chunks staged into the other half) once round j-1 is complete; `rp_drive` advances rounds j and j+1 together (ready-first service of the sender's puts in the rotated peer order, signal check and copy-out per arriving peer), so a slow pair of round j no longer blocks the ready pairs' writes of round j+1.
- Staging: two halves of `ROUND_MB/2/(n-1)` per pair (same total budget; pool law unchanged); `staging()` gets 2x the per-half size. The chunk halves, so a pair between ROUND_MB/2/(n-1) and ROUND_MB/(n-1) bytes takes 2 rounds (was 1). Every PE must run the same setting (chunk size and round count follow it). Digits identical by construction (same bytes, same destinations).
- Control words: round j's offset/signal words must not be overwritten by round j+2 before being read, and round j+1's offsets are published while round j's may be unread, so odd rounds use two new words `W_ROFF1`, `W_SIG1` (`put_signalled` selects by `p->pw`). 16 B per rank added to every communicator's control block. Even rounds and K=1 exchanges use the old words (interoperates pairwise with the plain rounds).
- Deadlock argument: every member publishes round j before blocking in round j-1's completion; round j+1 opens only after j-1 is complete at that member, which needs peers' signals of j-1, which need my j-1 offsets (published at my round j-1 open). Induction.
- Idle time while no pair is ready is booked in `xsig` (XSTATS); `xroff` is not split out under RPIPE.

## C5 `MN_REDIST_FUSE=1` (rns_dist.c, `redistribute_fused`, call sites in `mn_core`)
- For a product with a full miss of A and B (`ha < 0 && !bfull`) the three blocking redistributions (A, B, X) become one `comm_alltoallv`: per destination the segments `[A | B | X]` back to back (the pack kernels write at shifted `seg.off`), the receive buffer is that per-sender layout and each operand's sequence is copied out device-to-device into its own `rb` (the gathers read contiguous sequences, in node order, so they are unchanged). Partial hits, full hits and everything else take `redistribute` as before. All nodes decide alike (ha, hb, X are the product's).
- **Memory (modelled, not measured):** the XEFF row says 0; the P24 path frees A's sequence before B's, so the fused path raises the transient peak: all send buffers together + the combined receive buffer on top of the operands' own, about two planes (2 q x 8 B) per APU. The 4-node 6.441e10 runs of T09 had two memory-guard aborts already, so item 62 may abort with `ECALC_MEM_GUARD`; that is a result, not a bug. A cheaper variant would need segment-aware gather kernels (owner a08).
- Cost added: 2-3 x (g) device copies of the received bytes per product (D2D, ~1 TB/s class; modelled 10 ms per product per 7 GB operand), against the saved two blocking exchange start-ups.
