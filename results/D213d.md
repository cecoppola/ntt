## Headline (preliminary, 20:35 EDT — before G's sweep and L's table)

**4.4 × 10¹³ does NOT sit below the step in the model once the code's term layout is modelled.** The code gives every
node the same number of *terms* (mn.c: node r computes [1 + N r/g, 1 + N (r+1)/g)), not the same number of digits. A
node's Q is Σ log10 k over its terms, so at 576 nodes the **top node holds 1.0358 × the average** (node 0: 0.772;
modelled from lgamma, exact given the layout). The model before 13d costed every node at the average. Every tree level
waits for its slowest group (the top one, 1.034–1.036 × average up to the 64-groups) and the wall waits for the slowest
leaf (the top node's). With that:

| | old model (even shares) | 13d model (term shares) |
|---|---|---|
| the 2³¹ step (tree levels 8 → 12 pieces each; top node's leaf 4 → 6) | 4.45 → 4.46 × 10¹³ | **4.29 → 4.30 × 10¹³** (the leaf and levels 2–8 at 4.300, levels 16–64 by 4.308, the 192-level by 4.34) |
| 4.4 × 10¹³ | 214 pieces, below | 246 pieces, **2.3 % above** |

The mechanism is the size-1 step S4 (7.735 × 10¹⁰: the tree top nq/2 × nq/2 outgrows 2 × 2 pieces of 2³¹ at nq = 2³²
limbs = 7.731 × 10¹⁰ digits) reached by the top node at 7.731 × 10¹⁰ / 1.0358 × 576 = 4.30 × 10¹³ total. At 4.25 × 10¹³
the top node's leaf is 7.64 × 10¹⁰ digits — exactly the share run's size.

**Proposed new target: 4.25 × 10¹³** (1.2 % below the modelled step). To be confirmed by (a) G's size-1 sweep across S4
(the step's position at size 1) and G's size-4 test of the term shares (results/D2_13d_steps1.txt, the update), (b) L's
C-printed piece table if it reports the top group (see task 3 below).

## 2. The recalibration (in progress)

The 6.2 %: the design table's 129.9 s at 7.64e10 = node_phases × the design table's leaf_scale (measured / modelled at
4e10 = 0.949, applied to bs only). node_phases itself: 133.2 s = init 20.6 + bs 57.5 + dm 55.0, against measured 19.5 +
57.5 + 60.8. **The miss is all in dm (−9.5 %)**; bs matches exactly unscaled, but the leaf_scale (fitted at 4e10, where the
model's bs is 12 % high: 27.0 vs 23.7) took 3 s off it. Origin: node_phases is the Phase 13b model — K's kernels off, the
four-prime phase table (4e10 at 3·2³⁰, 8e10 and 1e11 from the Phase 11/12 code) extrapolated by the rest's law; its bs
grows too slowly from 4e10 (×2.13 to 7.64e10 against ×2.43 measured) and its dm's big products are the isolated
t_strategy pieces, which the pipeline runs ~10 % slower (dm ratio 1.02 at 4e10, 1.105 at 7.64e10, 1.098 at 1.3e11 K-on
equivalent: the big products are ~85 % of dm at ≥ 7e10).

Implemented (`CAL13`, mn_model.py): per phase group (init, bs, dm) the ratio measured / node_phases of the default
design at each measured size, linear in log D between the points, flat outside; K-off runs through the measured K ratios
(bs 0.933, dm 0.983 at 4e10, auto 2³¹, M-run n = 8 / 3). At g > 1: the bs ratio at the top node's leaf; the dm ratio at
7.64e10 (1.105) on the fabric pieces' local part (PIECE13, **assumed** to carry over). Points so far: 4e10 (n = 5),
7.64e10, 1.3e11 (P13b edge, K off). G's sweep will be added as it lands.
