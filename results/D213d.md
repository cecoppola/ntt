agrees with it piece for piece. The step is at **4.29 → 4.30 × 10¹³** (tree levels' largest groups 88 → 100 pieces, then

- **Why it moved**: the code gives every node the same number of *terms* (mn.c: node r computes [1 + N r/g, 1 + N (r+1)/g)),
  so a node's digits are Σ log10 k over its terms. At 576 nodes the **top node holds 1.0358 × the average** (node 0:
  0.772), the top groups of the binary levels 1.034–1.036 ×. Every level waits for its slowest (top) group and the wall
  for the top node's leaf. The old model (and the §80 table) costed node 0's group at the average: its 214 pieces at 4.4e13
  are exactly L's node-0 count (`total`); L's `total_max` is 229.
- **576-node estimate (modelled, the 13c defaults, CAL13 recalibration, fabric assumed at 100 GB/s per APU)**:
  4.25 × 10¹³ → **228 s (3.8 min)**, 452 GB per node; 4.29 → 229 s; 4.30 → 239 s; **4.4 × 10¹³ → 264 s (4.4 min)**,
  463 GB. Without the recalibration: 222 / 256 s.

  tree_max stays at 88 and recip / div at 69 / 28; its top node's leaf is 7.64 × 10¹⁰ digits, exactly the measured share
  run's size. (4.4e13 costs +36 s for +3.5 % digits.)
