  rule, as the task asked. Under that rule, tree products whose 18-digit pieces have min(pa, pb) ≤ the three-prime bound drop from P24
  (four primes at 24 digits, 3/4 of the points, fewer pieces) to three primes at 18 digits. The plan shows it: at 5.1 × 10¹³ the P24
  tree pieces fall 48 → 32 and node 0's pieces rise 166 → 174 (§3.2). Per point the two forms cost about the same (4 primes × 3/4 ≈ 3

- **Under `MN_P24=2` MPB changes no mn product**, because every mn product runs P24 at four primes. MPB then acts only in `dist_core`
  (the one-node tier and the leaf), where the model prices nothing (−0.0 s).

  against today's 400.2 s at cache 0.
