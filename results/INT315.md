## 2. The composition (every function touched)

### 2.1 MPB × P24: the prime count and the P24 decision

- **`rns_dist.c` `mn_core`**: `const int np = p24 ? 4 : ec_np_prod(ec_np_terms(nc, na, nb), bi_decimal, "mn_core");`. A P24 product is
  always four primes, as P24 had it. Any other mn product counts its terms as MPB does: min(na, nb) under `ECALC_NP_AUTO_MIN=1`, nc
  otherwise. With both switches off this is the same call as before, instruction for instruction (`ec_np_terms` returns nc).
- **`rns_dist.c` `mn_p24_of`** (P24's decision, used by `mn_grid`, `rns_mul_dist_mn_shape`, `rns_dist_mn_plan`,
  `rns_mul_dist_mn_scratch` and `rns_mul_dist_mn_stage`): it now computes the 18-digit grid's largest piece pa × pb. That is the whole
  product on one plane; `mn_grid_shape`'s grid's first piece; or P24's sizing guess of 1/32 beyond any grid (unchanged). It returns
  `ec_np_for(ec_np_terms(pa + pb, pa, pb)) == 4`, which is the test `mn_core`'s `ec_np_prod` makes on that piece. Under
  `ECALC_NP_AUTO_MIN=1` the test is min(pa, pb) > the switch-over (MPB's rule), else pa + pb > it (NP's, as before). `MN_P24=2`
  and `ECALC_NP=3/4` do not depend on the rule.
- **`mn_model.py`**:
  - `p24_of` makes the same decision on `split_grid`'s largest piece (min under `NP_AUTO_MIN`).
  - `piece_cost`: `npc = 4 if p24 else piece_np(dz, nc, na, nb)`.
  - `product_cost`'s memo key carries both `NP_AUTO_MIN` and `P24`, `P24_F`.
- **`mn_plan.c` `plan_run`**: MPB's `plan primes` line (with `np_by()`), then P24's `plan p24` line. `chk` (18-digit pieces, pa + pb or
  min as the run checks) and `chk24` (P24 pieces) are both kept, and `show_mn` picks one by `p.p24` as before.
- `rns_dist_mn_plan`'s P24 branch keeps `formed4 = formed`, `np = 4`. Its 18-digit branch carries MPB's two lines.

The decision can only move a product from P24 to 18 digits at three primes, never the other way: min(pa, pb) ≤ pa + pb. So with MPB
on, the P24 set is a subset of P24's own, and every P24 product keeps P24's grid (`p24_split`), guards and plane.

### 2.2 The bounds (the 24-digit version of MPB's argument)

MPB's argument (results/MPB15.md §2) is per coefficient: c_k = Σ a_i b_j over i + j = k has at most min(na, nb) terms. It carries
over to P24's points unchanged, with a point < 10²⁴ in place of a limb < 10¹⁸. The P24 plane holds the acyclic product (Pa + Pb ≤ the
plane, no wrap), so for Pa and Pb points c_k ≤ min(Pa, Pb)·(10²⁴ − 1)². With M₃ = p₀p₁p₂ and M₄ = p₀p₁p₂p₃ (the WP8 set, modarith.h;
exact integer arithmetic):

| form | exact iff min(·) ≤ | value |
|---|---|---|
| 18 digits, three primes (MPB's bound, `np3_max_terms`) | ⌊(M₃ − 1)/(10¹⁸ − 1)²⌋ limbs | 58 424 467 928 |
| 18 digits, four primes | ⌊(M₄ − 1)/(10¹⁸ − 1)²⌋ limbs | 1.51 × 10²⁶ |
| **24 digits, four primes (P24's Garner)** | ⌊(M₄ − 1)/(10²⁴ − 1)²⌋ points | **151 088 674 074 146 = 1.511 × 10¹⁴ = 2^47.10** |
| 24 digits, three primes | ⌊(M₃ − 1)/(10²⁴ − 1)²⌋ | **0**: no product, so a P24 product can never run three primes (`mn_core`'s `p24 ? 4 :`) |
| P24's CRT output in four limbs (c·10^s < 10⁷², s ≤ 12) | ⌊(10⁶⁰ − 1)/(10²⁴ − 1)²⌋ points | 10¹² (`P24_MIN_MAX`): **the binding bound** |

**P24's guards still hold with MPB, and do not depend on the prime rule:**
- `mn_core` stops on min(Pa, Pb) > 10¹² for a P24 piece.
- The P24 plane is at most 2⁴⁰ points (`P24_CAP_LOG`, `p24_cap`), so min ≤ 2³⁹ < 10¹².
- The CRT kernel flags a fifth limb.
- The plan's `chk24` checks both limits at plan time.

MPB only removes products from P24, so none of this is loosened. Every plan in §3 is `plan check … OK` (24 of 24), and the largest P24
piece at 5.1 × 10¹³ is 715 594 809 924 + 364 401 143 098 points (min 3.64 × 10¹¹ < 10¹², plan), the same with and without MPB.
At 5.55 × 10¹³ it is 740 743 651 797 + 314 351 269 642 points (min 3.14 × 10¹¹).

## 5. Summary, decisions, open issues

**The branch composes.** Each switch works alone and with the others, and the defaults are unchanged: the default plan is
byte-identical to int15h's, and `--check-c` is exact. The node results are in §4.

**For the user's decision (modelled, §0):**
1. **`MN_P24=2` + `NEWTON_DKM=1`** on the launch line (`ECALC_NP=auto`) is the fastest combination: 288.1 / 334.3 s at 5.1 × 10¹³,
   cache 0. `ECALC_NP_AUTO_MIN=1` makes no difference there (every mn product is P24). Adopting it is harmless and helps only the
   size-1 tier.
2. **Do not combine `MN_P24=1` with `ECALC_NP_AUTO_MIN=1` as composed here.** MPB's rule drops tree products from P24 back to 18 digits
