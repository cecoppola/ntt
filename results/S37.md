## S-6 HIP graphs: ceiling measured at 0.03 s (dropped)

Method: `rocprofv3 --kernel-trace --stats` on one node of hold 12454 (x9000c1s0b0n0, all 4 APUs in one ecalc process, ~/ntt-wt/s35 binary,
default switches, VERIFY OK both runs), counting every GPU kernel dispatch from the rocpd database (script `~/s37/q.py` on aac7; logs `~/s37/prof/`).

| run | wall (m) | kernel dispatches (m) | of them < 30 us GPU time (m) | GPU busy, summed over APUs (m) | dispatches x 4 us (3.5-4 us per dispatch, S-6 row) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1e9 | 13.1 s | 4709 | 2354 (25.5 ms GPU in total) | 1.68 s | 0.019 s |
| 1e10 | 26.8 s | 6110 | 2566 (28.1 ms GPU in total) | 17.1 s | 0.024 s |

- The dispatch count grows only about x1.3 for x10 digits (tiles and passes get larger, not more numerous). Extrapolated to 6.441e10: about 7 000 dispatches = about 0.03 s
  at 4 us each (modelled), and that is an upper bound on the saving because the launches are asynchronous and mostly hidden behind kernels of 0.1-50 ms.
- Batch-tier kernels average 0.3 ms (1e9) to 2-10 ms (1e10); only 2 500 of the dispatches are < 30 us and they total 0.03 s of GPU time.
  The "fuse kernels under 30 us" threshold is therefore not a lever either: the whole population is 28 ms.
- The note's assumption (upper bound about 4 s) would need about 10^6 dispatches; measured is 6 x 10^3. LEAF-tier work is CPU (the seed spans), not GPU dispatches.
- Verdict: ceiling 0.03 s << 1 s. Dropped; no ECALC_HIPGRAPH switch written.

## S-3 Barrett constant division for the decimal seeds: ceiling about 0.4 s of run wall (modelled; dropped)

- The item is largely already done: `BI_MUL1_FAST=1` is the default (Phase 15 S1, bigint.c `mul1_dec_fast`, `bi_span_step`): the per-limb quotient by 10^18 is a multiply by a
  precomputed reciprocal with two branch-free corrections (RESULTS 58: 4.4 -> 2.6 ns/limb; decimal seeds 22.4 -> 16.3 s). The "-10 s" row is the pre-S1 figure.
- What is left is one 128/64 division per series term k, in `recip_b10(k)` (`(m<<64)/10^18`, a `__udivti3` call). Micro-benchmark (gcc -O2, this workstation, m): 29.0 ns
  against 7.8 ns for a reciprocal-multiply Barrett form (floor(2^123/10^18) times m, >> 59, one correction loop; identical on 3 x 10^7 random and 12 600 strided inputs). Saving about 21 ns per term.
- Terms at 6.441e10 digits: k(log10 k - 0.434) = 6.44e10 gives about 6.9e9 terms, spread over 192 threads: 6.9e9 x 21 ns / 192 = about 0.75 s of seed-thread time (modelled; Zen 4 on the
  node may differ by tens of percent).
- Run coupling: S22 section 3.1 (measured, n = 2 each, one node): 2 s slower seed spans moved the total by about 0 (<= 0.5 s per s); seed and mapping co-bind. So 0.75 s of seed time is at most 0.4 s of wall, far inside the noise (sd 1.4 s).
- Verdict: ceiling < 1 s. Dropped; no BS_SEED_BARRETT switch written. (If wanted later: a 15-line change confined to `recip_b10`.)
