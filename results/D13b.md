## 3. `design_table.py`

- The rows: `mn_model.STRATEGIES × CAPS × CHUNKS × depth` = 96, each at `ECALC_NP=3 NTT_MODMUL=1`.
- Per row it gives (a) and (b), size 1 at 4 × 10¹⁰; (c) `max_digits(576, 502 / 480, design)`; (d) the run at the
  502-GB maximum; (e) the run at 4 × 10¹³ / 576 per node; (f) (e) at the low and high bandwidths (`--bws`, default
  50,100,200).
- The Pareto front is taken on ((e), (c) at 502) over the rows that hold 4 × 10¹³. F is the minimum (e); L is the
  maximum (c); R is the front row that maximises (its safe digits / L's) + (F's wall / its wall).
- Measured replacement (`--mrun`):
  - A (strategy, cap) with size-1 4 × 10¹⁰ runs at three primes and `NTT_MODMUL=1` gets its (a) and (b) measured.
  - Its 576-node leaf is scaled by measured / modelled (`leaf_scale`), and its ceilings use the budget less any
    measured peak excess.
  - Runs at one size and g that differ only in chunking refit `T_ROUND`.
  - A depth pair at a power-of-two size is reported as not exercising the switch.
  - A line with DIFFERS / FAILED is reported and not used.
- Options: `--lat`, `--write-bw`, `--hide-pow2`, `--gen-hide2`, `--e0` (a further t_strategy log), `--quick` (12 rows),
  `--make-line LOG KEY=V ...`.
- Tested:
  - `--calibrate` on the built-in runs: PASS.
  - A synthetic M-run log made from real runs (P3 b4 / b6, I, M13 b1 lines, one marked DIFFERS). The chunk lines refit
    T_ROUND to 0.082 s. The DIFFERS line is excluded. Two `ECALC_PLANE_CAP=2^31` lines at 68.5 s mean became a measured
    row, whose 576-node cells were scaled by 1.017 and whose ceiling dropped by the 13 GB peak excess.
  - `--make-line` on P3's 4 × 10¹⁰ log.
  - `--bws 40,80,160`.
  - `mn_model.py --calib` (legacy) unchanged: worst 6.1 %.
  - `estimate.py --legacy --max` unchanged: 6.95 × 10¹⁰, 4.3 min.

## M-RUN SWITCHES

D has no runtime switch (no C). What the M-run should give the table:

1. **One line per run** in the format `design_table.py` reads (`--make-line` writes it from a log):
   ```
   digits=40000000000 size=1 RNS_STRATEGY=<C|B|B4|auto> ECALC_PLANE_CAP=<2^30|3*2^29|2^31|3*2^30> [MDB_SHIFT_CHUNK_MB=.. MN_T_CHUNK_MB=.. COMM_ALLTOALLV_DEPTH=.. DIST_GEN=1] | <ecalc's total line> | device <mem summary's init device total> GB identical|DIFFERS
   ```
   One way is `./design_table.py --make-line <log> size=1 RNS_STRATEGY=B ECALC_PLANE_CAP=2^31 >> mrun.log`. ECALC_NP is
   read from the log (rns_init prints "three primes"); NTT_MODMUL is taken as 1 unless given. Run the 4 × 10¹⁰ runs with
   `MEM_REPORT_DEVS=1` or plain: the `mem summary` is printed either way.
2. **The size-1 block** (strategy × cap, three runs each): these become columns (a) and (b) and scale the 576-node leaf.
   A row the M-run does not cover stays modelled (for example B at 3·2³⁰, 450 GB of planes).
3. **The 10¹⁰ over 4 processes block**: chunking {off, shift, both} at one chunk size refits T_ROUND. For **depth**, 4
   node-processes are a power of two and never use the general map. Give both depth rows `DIST_GEN=1` (as X13b says), or
   run 3 processes; otherwise the depth pair is reported as uninformative. The depth's hidden fraction is better taken
   from X's `COMM_LAYER_STATS` both-busy numbers (`--gen-hide2`).
4. Then `./design_table.py --calibrate --mrun mrun.log` (gate) and `./design_table.py --mrun mrun.log` (the table).

## Open issues

- **cap_factor.** The pipeline's big products at POOL_LOG 30 run 1.30–1.39 × the per-product law, and the mechanism is
  not identified. It is fitted on one run per cap. At POOL_LOG 31 the law alone matches (I's 2³¹ runs, ≤ 0.3 s per
  phase). The candidates: the mdev tier's pieces at 3·2²⁸ with three primes, pool 1 at the batch tile, and
  `DIST_LOGN_TEST=30` set by `ECALC_PLANE_CAP`. Whether the same factor applies to the mn tier's pieces at 576 is not
  known; the model applies it only to the leaf's single-node products.
- **Three primes at 8 × 10¹⁰ and 10¹¹ are not measured.** The factor comes from the 4 × 10¹⁰ series. P's 10¹¹ edge runs,
  or the M-run, would check it. The four-prime 10¹¹ point is Phase 11 code.
- **T_ROUND on the target.** It is fitted on loopback TCP at ±100 %, and it decides how much the chunked rows cost (§6
  item 4 of TARGET.md).
- **Depth 2.** The hidden fraction (0.75) is modelled. X13b's both-busy measurement replaces it (`--gen-hide2`).
- **The leave-one-out error of the size-1 phase table** is up to 11 % between table points. That matters only for D
  between the measured sizes. The 576 leaf at 5–10 × 10¹⁰ is bracketed by the 4e10, 8e10 and 1e11 points.
- **Host at size > 1** stays the Phase 12 constants (6 GB per process + the SHMEM pool; M13 open issue 1).
- **The B forms' extra planes are charged to init** at the mapping rate. In the code they are a hipMalloc in the phase,
  the same cost at another time.
