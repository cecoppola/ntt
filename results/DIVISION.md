## Topic summary

- **What this is.** How the quotient e = P/Q-style division at the end of the binary-splitting phase is computed (`newton_db.c`: `newton_mn_divmod` over shares at size > 1, `newton_db_divmod_shifted` at size 1): Newton reciprocal by an anchored doubling chain, a high product A_h·μ, a low product X·Q mod B^w for the remainder, and a correction loop (≤ 64 steps, fatal beyond; digits are exact whenever a run completes, the corrections make X exact). dm is the largest multi-node cost (+94 s at 6 nodes vs 1, m; §P1).
- **Current state on main (ecalc/README.md, RESULTS.md, `switches.tsv`).** Built in and no longer switches: DKM is the only division (§DKM15: dm −25.7 s at 10¹¹ one node, m), the reciprocal's cut and middle product (§R114 E7, §R415; −3.4 s of the reciprocal, −3.2 s dm at 10¹¹, m). Defaults: `MN_OUT_DKM_HI` 1 (§EW15, writer starts on X_hi after step 1), `ECALC_CORR_PATCH` 2 (§K15), `NEWTON_CHAIN_GUARD` 1 (user decision D25, 2026-10-13; §RECIP), `DM_MN_WRAP` automatic by the memory fit (D26, 2026-10-13; §P1), `DBIG_SMALL_BOUNCE` 1 (2026-10-12; §O2), `DB_POOL_VMM` and `DM_TIGHT` derived by the planner (§R114). Dropped / not built: B5 (§O5, no gain, removed from the code), E9 Karatsuba and E10b disk spill (§R114, rejected by the model), E11 `DM_BAND` and the band-sized stores (§M615, stopped), B2 transform cache (§A-div, not implemented).
- **Live conclusions.** (1) The products of the division are exchange-bound; fewer transforms for the same information is the lever (cyclic low product §P1: −7.7 s of dm at 2 nodes, m; DKM: one doubling fewer). (2) The reciprocal's error is O(M) units with M = B^n_Q/Q ∈ [1, B]; the `t_newton` bound is `recip_bound`, not ±8; runs of exact doublings (k = 2^m) square the error and can make DKM step 1 fatal, which the guard limb removes (§RECIP). (3) The exactness of every cut rests on the correction loop or on a reduction mod B^w; read the argument in the item before changing a cut. (4) The band product (`rns_mul_band_db`) is bit-identical to the cut path because the dropped pieces are multiples of B^w (§R415). (5) Memory: at the 576 target the dm need binds (244.7 GB arena, tree floor 224.8, at most 19.9 GB per node saveable, mod, §M615); DKM saves node memory only with a DKM-aware `dm_layout` (−5.1 GB, mod, §DKM15). (6) The first-copy pinning stall (8 GiB of pinned bounce buffers) cost 2.2 s per node and up to 10 s on the slow rank of the reciprocal's gather (§O2).
- **Superseded / historical.** A-div's flow and tests are the base; the host-A `newton_db_divmod` is not production; EW15's and K15's 10¹¹ timing series are one-node results on aac6; §A-div's "X gathered to node 0" and "low product over shares is the full X·Q" were replaced by A-out and the grid over shares. The §4.3 packed tail-patch defect found in §DKM15 was a bug of main fixed later (see `results/OUTPUT_IO.md`).
- **Open (per item):** 2-node gate and ABBA for `NEWTON_CHAIN_GUARD` were closed by its adoption; the 6-node ABBA of `DM_MN_WRAP`; the 2-node `ECALC_LIVE=1` measurement of the mn dm peak (§M615); the part-file count 2·size under `MN_OUT_DKM_HI` for target scripts (§EW15).

## A-div — M4: the distributed division, with B2/B3/C3 (Phase 9, PLAN.md §19)

Branch `adiv-m4` (from main a75474d); owns `ecalc/newton_db.c` and the dm flow of `ecalc.c`; minimal touches in `dbig.c/.h` (two fixed-length share ops), `newton.h`, `mdb.h`, `Makefile`.

**Design.** After `mn_tree` the final P, Q are `mdb` over the top group G = [0, size). dm runs over G (`newton_mn_divmod`, default; `MN_DM=host` keeps M3's gather-to-node-0 flow); X stays an `mdb` and is gathered to node 0 for the output (A-out later replaced this). Size 1 goes through none of it. Every big product is `rns_mul_dist_mn` over G; the rest is share-level primitives in `newton_db.c`: `mdb_shift` (one all-to-all per APU thread over the group's mesh, pieces = source/target share intersections, truncation re-normalised by `allreduce_max`), `mdb_addsub` (fixed-length share add plus a node carry scan and a second pass on nodes receiving a carry), `mdb_add_val` (X corrections), `mdb_cmp/limb/nonzero_below/norm/pow`, `mdb_mod_qs` (residues mod ≤ 16 primes), `mdb_to_host_all` / `mdb_from_db/bi` (small numbers on every host).

**Reciprocal (`recip_mn`).** The anchored doubling chain (k, ⌈k/2⌉, …) up to the largest target ≤ `NEWTON_MN_SPLIT` (2¹⁶ limbs) runs on every node identically with the single-node `recip_db` on Q's top 2kp + 2 limbs; each node then takes its share of r and the remaining steps are the correction-form step over shares (Q_t = Q >> …, t = Q_t r, u, d = |B^{2j} − u|, corr = (r d) >> j, r' = r << j ∓ corr; the overshoot shrink r −= r/16 goes through the hosts, rare). Per step 2 products, ≈ 6 shifts, 2 adds. **Division** mirrors `newton_db_divmod_shifted`: residues of P, Q; μ with k_μ from S's largest length; S = P + Q; A_h = S >> (n_Q − 1 − dl); t = A_h μ; X = t >> (k + 1); the low product X·Q as the full product truncated to w = n_Q + 2; window and ±Q corrections by `mdb_cmp`/`mdb_addsub`; X ± dx by `mdb_add_val`; R's residues by `mdb_mod_qs`.

**B3 (single node)** `mul_high_db`: the A_h·μ product as a grid of piece products skipping every piece ending at or below the cut k + 1; at 4 × 10¹⁰ the 2 × 3 grid loses one of six 2³¹-point products; `NEWTON_HIGHPROD=0` restores the full product. Not yet over shares. **C3** `ECALC_DM_POOL=1`: grow the block pool once by the reciprocal's scratch deficit before the phase; off by default. **B2 (not implemented):** sharing a transform cache of Q's pieces between the last doubling's Q_t·r and X·Q needs 16 GiB per APU per piece (48 GiB for three, on top of ≈ 62 GB per APU) and an optional transform-cache argument in `dist_core`/`mul_grid` (`rns_mul_dist_db_cached`); expected −3…−5 s of dm at 4 × 10¹⁰ (PLAN I4, mod). Interface note for the grid over shares: `lowcut` and `highcut` parameters (`rns_mul_dist_mn_cut`), later implemented (see §R415).

## RECIP — the Newton reciprocal's error: the t_newton bound or the reciprocal?

**Question.** `t_newton` found one divisor Q whose reciprocal μ ≈ B^(n_Q+k)/Q was −22 units off GMP's floor at n_Q 131075, k 65538, beyond the ±8 bound (`results/CODE_REDUCTION.md §SIMP`). Bound wrong, or reciprocal? Can it make a division wrong? Branch `recip`; switch `NEWTON_CHAIN_GUARD` (default 0): one guard limb per doubling in the anchored chain; `t_newton`'s bound is now the derived `recip_bound`, plus a new section 8.

**Answer.**
1. **The −22 is the bound's fault.** The ±8 assumes a normalised Q; with decimal limbs one unit of μ is relative to M = B^n_Q/Q ∈ [1, B]. SIMP's Q has top limb 2.349·10¹⁶ (M = 42.6) and its last doubling 32769 → 65538 is exact (no limb cut afterwards), which keeps its rounding error, at most 2M + 3 units; −22 = −0.52·M. A host GMP model of `recip_db2` with exact products reproduces −22 bit for bit and SIMP's three rng5 errors (+1, +0, +1).
2. **A real weakness the test never reached.** Several exact doublings in a row (⌈k/2^i⌉ even for consecutive i, e.g. k = 2^m) square the error left by the previous one (ε → −ε² + rounding): modelled errors up to 10¹²–10¹⁹ units (one limb) for some Q with M ≈ 1, 10³⁵ (two limbs) at h = 2048. The division's corrections absorb ~64·B units; beyond that DKM step 1 dies with `DKM step 1: N corrections` (fatal; never wrong digits). Modelled: for a 4095-limb quotient (h = k₁ = 2048) 3 of 900 step-1 estimates are off by > 64 (up to 1.8·10⁸). Production exposure (mod, order of magnitude): top run of ≥ 8 exact doublings for ~0.4 % of k; fatal needs a long run, h = k₁ and an unlucky Q, ≈ 10⁻⁵ per run size; at 10⁹…10¹² digits h is odd (top run 0).
3. **Fix: one guard limb per level** (`NEWTON_CHAIN_GUARD=1`): targets k, ⌈k/2⌉ + 1, … so every doubling has jn ≤ 2j − 1 and is cut by ≥ 1 limb. Modelled error 0 in every sweep; ≤ 2 extra tiny doublings at the bottom (≤ 19 instead of 17 up to k = 2·10⁵), negligible. Digits cannot change (the division corrects to the exact quotient either way).

**Derivation (one doubling, correction form):** r' − Y = −Y e² + (r/B^j) γ ± (β₂ + c₂) with γ ∈ [0, 2 + 1/B), r/B^j ≈ M, Y e² = δ²/M. If jn < 2j the M-scaled term (≤ 2M + 3) is cut to ≤ 3 units; if jn = 2j nothing cuts it: err ≥ −(2⌈M⌉ + 4), and the next doubling turns δ ≈ 2M into 4M; in units ε = δ/M a run of exact doublings maps ε → −ε² + γ, diverging at |ε| ≥ 2 (M ≈ 1). The convergence check (corr < B^(j+1)) only catches |ε| M ≥ B.

**Method.** Local C/GMP model of `recip_db2` and `dkm_est_db` (`archive:drivers/recip/rmodel.c`), replaying SIMP's random stream; GPU on aac7, 1 node (item `50_recip_gpu`, job 12775, e475e29; rebased 24446df, item `51_recip_rebased`).

**Results** (mod unless marked): SIMP's Q: error −22 off, 0 on; sweep n_Q 1027, k 514 (one exact doubling) −174…+1 (exact products) / −479…+2 (cuts), max |err|/M 2.47, guard on 0; k = 512 (9 exact doublings) up to 3.2·10¹² (exact) / 7.3·10¹⁸ (cuts), guard 0; k = 1024 / 2048 up to 4.5·10¹³ / 1.1·10¹² (exact), ~10¹⁸ (cuts); odd k (513, 1027) 0; derived bound −(2⌈M⌉ + 6)…8 with no two exact doublings in a row: 0 violations in 2 × 3 × 300 Q; DKM step 1 (n_Q 8200, h = k₁ = 2048, 300 Q × 3 A): |X₁ − X₀| up to 1.8·10⁸, 3 of 900 beyond 64, guard 0 of 900; quotient 2047 / 8191 3 / 9 of 900 beyond 64; top runs 1…7: μ error 84…4.5·10⁷ units, X₀ within 1.

GPU (m): `t_newton 20` guard off VERIFY OK (2206 checks; after rebase 2756 checks, section 8 identical); guard on VERIFY OK (2756 checks, all sections); 8(a) SIMP's Q −22 units (bound [−92, 8]) off, **0** on; 8(b) n_Q 1027, k 1024, 100 uniform Q: **15** beyond ±8, largest 9.9·10²⁴ units off, 0 on; k 2048: **9** beyond ±8, largest 2.0·10²⁷, 0 on; 8(c) DKM step 1 on the device μ: max |X₁ − X₀| 1.83·10⁸, 3 of 900 beyond 64 (the model's numbers exactly), 0 on, the 6 fatal-size divisions exact against GMP; gate 1 node 10⁹: PASS both (total 14.45 s off, 11.82 s on; one run each, not a timing comparison). The larger device errors at k = 2^m (10²⁴ vs the model's 10¹³ with exact products) come from the low cuts (model cut modes give up to ~10¹⁹ at n_Q 1027).

**Verdict.** (a) The −22 is a bound problem; the bound now scales with M (`recip_bound`: −(2⌈M⌉ + 6)…8 when the last doubling is exact, ±8 otherwise, none with two exact doublings in a row). (b) The reciprocal has a real, rare, fatal-never-wrong failure mode at long runs of exact doublings; `NEWTON_CHAIN_GUARD=1` removes it at no measurable cost (mod; 1-node gate passes, m). Changes no digits. Adoption is the user's. **Open:** the 2-node gate with the switch on (sharded chain `recip_mn_body`, `newton_mn_chain_start`) was not run, needed before a default flip; merge rebased onto SH3 + K4c (dfac293) not re-gated; `mem_model.py` does not mirror the +1 limb of `dm_layout`'s jl; the fatal case was not provoked through the division on the GPU. Merged to main (switch off); to adopt: 2-node gate `--env NEWTON_CHAIN_GUARD=1`, `mnaccept.sh --only unit,e9,mn`, a 1-node 10¹⁰ ABBA.

## R415 — R4 (Phase 15, row 1b): the reciprocal's Q_t·r as a middle product (V3 item 4 = PLAN §33 B2)

Branch `p15-R4` (from int15 0b80ca3). Files: `newton_db.c` (switch, `recip_db2`, `recip_mn`), `tests/t_newton.c` (section 5), README row; helpers `rns_mul_band_db` (`mul_grid` with both cuts truncated to w limbs) in `rns_dist.c`/`rns_mul.h`, `newton.h` stats and test hook, `mn_plan.c` (plan printer follows the switch). Switch `NEWTON_RECIP_MID` (a828807 and follow-ups; off by default, adoption is the user's).

**Exactness argument (written before the runs).** A round of `recip_db2`/`recip_mn` at precision j reads u = ⌊t1/B^v⌋ (v = take − j, take = min(2j + 2, n_Q)) only through (neg, d) = sign and magnitude of e = u − B^{2j}. The band product `rns_mul_band_db(C, A, B, c, w)`, w = take + 4 (`MID_HI` = 4), skips pieces with oa + ob ≥ w (multiples of B^w) and pieces below the low cut; so C = (t1 − S) mod B^w exactly (reduction mod B^w is a ring homomorphism, carries only move upward), and U = ⌊C/B^v⌋ = u_cut mod B^{j+4}. For j ≥ 4, U ≡ e mod B^{j+4}, so e is fixed by U if |e| < B^{j+3}. **Claim:** if the previous round did not overshoot and had d_p < B^{j_p+1} (`mid_ok`), then |e| < 5 B^{j+2} + 2 < B^{j+3} (proof by tracking the iterate's error through one round, the floor cuts, the shift to the next target and the larger `take`; B ≥ 6). `mid_d` takes e = U if U has ≤ j + 3 limbs, else U − B^{j+4}, and aborts if d exceeds j + 3 limbs (never a silent wrong u). Consequence: (neg, d), hence r', every later round and **μ are bit-identical** to the switch off, with the cut on or off (stronger than R114's cut). The whole product is formed (`mid_ok` = 0) for the first round after the seed, after an overshoot, after a round with d ≥ B^{j_p+1}, for `recip_mn`'s first sharded round and **the first round after the step's group changes** (added in a828807, see batch B), and for j < 4 or j > take.

**What is saved** (plan printer, decisions of the code on predicted sizes; m): top doubling's Q_t·r pieces formed, cut only → cut + middle: 10¹¹ on 1 node (9 × 3 grid) 24 → **20** (recip pieces 97 → 93); 1.3 × 10¹¹ (6 × 4) 21 → **18**; 4.25 × 10¹³ on 576 (5 × 2) 9 → **8** (67 → 66). Matches V3's model. Not built: V3's wrapped one-plane form (−0.5 s at 10¹¹, mod).

**Tests** (m; aac6): batch A (job 21463, 57c897e): t_newton binary/decimal at cap 2²⁰, cut off, real cap, switch off all VERIFY OK (906 checks), t_mul VERIFY OK (189); e9 decimal/binary (also at cap 2²⁴), 10¹⁰ (also cap 2²⁸) digits identical to the references (total 10.5 / 20.9 s at 10⁹; 24.0 s at 10¹⁰); 10¹¹ sha1 identical to the reference, total 238.4 s, top doubling 8 skipped / 27 formed (4 / 31 off). ### E7 — the reciprocal's products cut to the band the step reads
**Exactness.** A step at precision j reads u = t1 >> (take − j) (product 1) and corr = (r d) >> j (product 2). With ρ = B^take/Q_t ∈ (1, B] and E = R_j − r the input error, exactly r' = R_{2j} − E²/ρ + rθ/B^j − φ (θ, φ the floor fractions, 0 ≤ rθ/B^j < ρ): the un-cut step already errs by up to ρ + 1 units, the chain's steady state is |E| ≤ ρ + 1 and the final μ is off by O(ρ) units (`t_newton` measures ≤ 8 units, m). The division tolerates it: X moves by ≤ ⌈Δμ/B⌉ + 1, absorbed by the ±Q corrections (≤ 64, abort beyond; digits exact whenever the run completes). `mul_grid` with `lowcut = c` skips exactly pieces with oa + ob + la + lb ≤ c, so t1_cut = t1 − S with S < n_skip·B^c; cutting at c = v − g (g ≥ 1 guard limbs) gives ⌊t1_cut/B^v⌋ = ⌊t1/B^v⌋ − δ, δ ∈ {0, 1}, δ = 1 with probability < n_skip/B ≈ 10⁻¹⁵ per product, so u and corr are in practice bit-identical. Under the cut r'_cut = r'_exact + (rδ₁/B^j − δ₂), |Δ| ≤ B + 1: the same kind and size as the floor already made. Decisions: `converged` can only flip repeat → converged (harmless); the overshoot test is closed in the code (`r B^j ≤ corr_cut + 1`, `shifted_cmp_le`'s cut form); the chain does not depend on values. Healing: the cut acts only in the top m doublings that are grids (m = 1 at 4·10¹⁰…7.6·10¹⁰, 2 at 10¹¹, 3 at 1.3·10¹¹); after m cut steps |μ_cut − μ_exact| ≤ (16ρ + 90)B^{−s} + 1 worst case, ≤ 1 + (ρ + 1)B^{−s} when every δ = 0. So ΔX ≤ 2 (ρ ≤ B/32), ≤ 18 worst case (top limb < 32, decimal): digits unchanged, correction count +2 in practice. `recip_mn` uses `rns_mul_dist_mn_cut` with the same rule (`mn_prod_cut`).

## EW15: the writer starts on X_hi's digits after DKM's step 1 (Phase 15 Batch 3, agent EW)

Branch `p15-EW` (from int15i ab8daa2 = main + P24 + MPB + DKM + MS's fixes; defaults `MN_P24=2`, `NEWTON_DKM=1`); the follow-up of §DKM15's early-writer note, approved by the user 2026-09-29. Switch **`MN_OUT_DKM_HI=1`** (off in the base; current default in the Topic summary).

**Problem.** With DKM the early writer's hook sat before step 2's low product, so step 2's high product, its window and the assembly (7.4 s at 10¹¹ on one node, m; ≈ 7 s at the target, mod) ran with the writer idle.

**Proof that X_hi is final after step 1.** Step 1's loop stops only at 0 ≤ R₁ < Q, so A₂ = R₁·B^s < Q·B^s and X_lo < B^s; hence X = X_hi·B^s + X_lo and X's limbs at and above s are exactly X_hi's: nothing after step 1 changes them. Step 2's corrections change only X_lo (X_lo₀ + dx, in [0, B^s)). Carry case: X_lo₀ can exceed the truth by a few units, so X_lo₀ ≥ B^s when X_lo ≥ B^s − 64 (probability ≈ 64/B^s, never at s ≥ 10⁶ limbs); then every byte of the low half would change, so the corrections are applied to X_lo₀ in place first (no deferral) and the low half is written once from X_lo. Without a carry the deferred tail patch (`mn_out_tail_fix`) applies unchanged.
